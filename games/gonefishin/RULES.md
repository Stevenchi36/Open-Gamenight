# Gone Fishin'

**Goal:** collect the most **books**: all four cards of one rank, like four sevens.

## Setting up

Everyone is dealt **7 cards** with 2-3 players, or **5 cards** with 4-6. The rest go face down in the middle as the **pond**. You see your own hand; for everyone else you see only how many cards they hold.

## Your turn

1. **Ask.** Tap a rank in your hand, then a player with cards, then **Ask**. You can only ask for a rank you already hold.
2. **They have some:** they hand over **all** their cards of that rank, and you **ask again**.
3. **They don't:** "Gone fishin'!" You draw the top card of the pond.
   - **Lucky catch:** if it's the rank you asked for, you show it and ask again.
   - Otherwise your turn ends and the next player round the table goes.

## Books

As soon as you hold all four of a rank, the book goes down in front of you by itself. Books dealt in your starting hand go down too.

## Running out

- If your hand runs out, you draw a card from the pond and keep playing.
- If your hand and the pond are both empty, you're done for this game.
- If nobody else has any cards, you draw from the pond instead of asking, and your turn ends.

## Scoring

The game ends when all 13 books are down. Whoever has the most books wins **1 point**; tied players each get 1. Scores carry on from game to game.

## Next game

The host starts a new game. Everyone keeps their seat, and the first turn moves on one seat.

## Settings

- **Lucky catch** (default on): drawing the rank you asked for lets you ask again. Off: a miss always ends your turn.
- **Move history** (default **Last 5**): how many past asks you can look back at under **Moves this game**: **Off** (only the latest, so it's all memory), **Last 5**, or **All**.

## Tips

- Every ask is said out loud: if someone asks for queens, they hold a queen. Use that later.
- The table shows how many cards each player has; you can't ask someone with none.
- **Moves this game** lists recent asks (as many as Move history allows), if you lose track.
- If a player leaves mid-game, their cards go back into the pond and their books stay down.
