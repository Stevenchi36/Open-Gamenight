# Word Imposter

**Goal:** find the imposter, or, if you are the imposter, don't get caught.

## A round

1. **Get your word.** Hold your card to see it. Everyone gets the same secret word, except one random player: **the imposter**, who only sees the category (and maybe a hint).
2. **Give clues.** Take turns giving **one word** linked to the secret word, typed or said out loud (the host picks). Don't make it too obvious, or the imposter learns the word. The imposter bluffs along.
3. Clues go around the table one or more times (the host sets how many).
4. **Vote.** Everyone, the imposter included, secretly votes for who they think the imposter is. You can't vote for yourself.
5. **Reveal.** The player with the most votes is accused. If that's the imposter, they're **caught**. A wrong pick or a tie means the imposter **escapes**.
6. **Guess** (if the host turned it on). The imposter guesses the secret word. A judge (usually the host) taps **Got it** or **Missed it**.

## Scoring

- Imposter caught: every other player scores (default 1 point each).
- Imposter escapes: the imposter scores (default 2 points).
- Guess mode **Bonus**: a correct guess gives the imposter an extra point, caught or not.
- Guess mode **Steal**: a caught imposter who guesses the word wins the round instead.

The host can play more rounds; scores carry on, and each round has a new word and a new random imposter.

## Settings

- **Rotations:** how many times clues go around (1 to 5).
- **Clue mode:** typed (one word each) or spoken (say it out loud, then tap Done).
- **Imposter gets:** nothing, the category, or the category and a hint.
- **Category** and **word source:** which words are used.
- **Clue timer:** optional time limit per turn.
- **Imposter's guess:** bonus, steal or off, and the points for each outcome.

## Tips

- Typed clues can't be the secret word or repeat an earlier clue.
- Never show your screen: the word only appears while you hold your card.
- The screen glows when it's your turn.
- If you join mid-round, you play from the next round.
