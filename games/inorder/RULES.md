# In Order

**Goal:** work together as a team. Put everyone's secret numbers in order from their clues, without ever saying a number.

## A round

1. **Read the theme.** Everyone sees the same scale, like "How scary is it?", with what 1 and 100 mean (*Not scary at all* to *Terrifying*).
2. **Look at your number.** You get a secret number from 1 to 100 (or more than one, if the host chose it). Nobody else has the same number. Hold the card to see it.
3. **Give a clue.** Name something that fits the theme at your number: low numbers get "a kitten", high numbers get "a shark". **Never say your number**: a clue with your number in it is refused. Everyone sees the clues as they arrive, and you can change yours until the reveal.
4. **Put the line in order.** Every number is a card in one shared line, lowest at the top. Talk it over and drag the cards (or use the arrows) until the order feels right. Anyone can move any card.
5. **Ready.** When you're happy with the order, tap **Ready**. Any move or clue change clears everyone's Ready, so the table has to agree on the final order. Once everyone is ready (or the host taps **Reveal now**), the numbers turn over one by one.

## Mistakes and lives

- The team shares its **lives** (3 by default).
- A round's mistakes are the **fewest cards you'd have to move** to put the line right, and those cards are marked wrong. One card placed far from where it belongs is one mistake.
- Each mistake costs **1 life**.

## Winning

- **Rounds** (5 by default): clear them all with at least one life left and the team wins. Run out of lives and the team loses.
- **Until out of lives:** play on as long as you can. Your score is the number of rounds cleared.
- Perfect rounds (no mistakes) are counted too. The host can start a new game at the end.

## Settings

- **Rounds:** 3, 5, 7 or 10, or until out of lives.
- **Lives:** 1 to 5.
- **Numbers each:** 1, 2 or 3. More numbers make the line longer, which is good for small groups.
- **Themes from:** all (least-played first), or recent (the newest themes added first, then older ones).
- **Category:** any, or one category of themes.

## Tips

- Think about where your thing sits for most people, not only for you.
- With two or three numbers each, give your clues some distance from each other.
- Keep your screen hidden: your number only shows while you hold it.
- The screen glows when the game is waiting for your clue or your Ready.
- If you join mid-round, you play from the next round.
