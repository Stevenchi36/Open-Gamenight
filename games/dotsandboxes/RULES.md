# Dots and Boxes

**Goal:** claim more boxes than anyone else.

## A game

1. 2 to 4 players, each with a colour: **red**, **blue**, **green**, **orange**. Red goes first, then the turn goes round in that order.
2. On your turn, tap between two dots next to each other (across or down) to draw a line there.
3. If your line closes the fourth side of a box, the box is yours and you **draw again**. One line can close two boxes at once.
4. If your line doesn't close a box, the turn passes on.
5. When every line is drawn, whoever has the most boxes wins.

## Scoring

The winner scores **1 point**. Players tied for the most boxes each score 1 point. Scores carry on from game to game.

## Next game

The host starts a new game. The first turn (and red) moves on one player each game.

## Settings

- **Grid:** 3×3 up to 7×7 boxes (default 5×5).

## Tips

- Avoid drawing the third side of a box: the next player closes it and goes again.
- Near the end, the board splits into chains of boxes. Taking a whole chain can mean opening the next one for your opponent, so sometimes it's better to leave the last two boxes of a chain for them and keep control.
- The latest line drawn is highlighted, and the screen glows when it's your turn.
- If a player loses connection with 3 or more in the game, their turn is skipped after a short wait. With 2, it waits for them.
- If a player leaves, their boxes stay on the board but they can't win. With fewer than 2 players left, the game ends with no winner.
