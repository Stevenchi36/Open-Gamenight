# Last Card

**Goal:** be the first to play every card in your hand.

## The cards

108 cards in four colours (red, yellow, green, blue): numbers 0-9, and three action cards in each colour: **Skip**, **Reverse** and **Draw Two**. Plus black **Wild** and **Wild Draw Four** cards.

## Setting up

Everyone is dealt **7 cards**. The rest go face down as the **draw pile**, and the top card is turned up to start the discard pile. If it's an action card, it works as if the player before the first player had played it (a Reverse means they go first, the other way round).

## Your turn

1. **Play a card** that matches the top card's **colour** or its **number or symbol**: a red 7 goes on any red card or any 7. Tap it, then **Play**.
2. **Wilds** go on anything; you pick the colour to play next. A **Wild Draw Four** only when you have no card of the colour in play.
3. **Or draw** a card (you may, even if you could play). If the card you drew fits, you can play it straight away, or **Keep it**. Either way your turn ends.

When the draw pile runs out, the discard pile (all but its top card) is shuffled into a new one.

## Action cards

- **Skip:** the next player misses their turn.
- **Reverse:** play changes direction. With two players it works like a Skip.
- **Draw Two:** the next player draws 2 and misses their turn.
- **Wild Draw Four:** pick a colour; the next player draws 4 and misses their turn.

## Last card!

When you play your second-to-last card, press **Last card!** before you play it. Forget, and anyone can **Catch** you before the next card is played or drawn: you draw 2. You can still press it late, if you beat them to it.

## Scoring

The first to play their last card wins the round. If it was a draw card, the next player still draws.

- **Points** (default): the winner scores the cards left in everyone else's hands: number cards their number, Skip, Reverse and Draw Two 20, wilds 50. The first to reach the target (500 by default) wins the game.
- **Rounds:** each round won is 1 point.

The host deals the next round. Everyone keeps their seat, and the first turn moves on one seat.

## Settings

- **Scoring** (default Points) and **Target** (100, 200, 300 or 500).
- **Last card call** (default Call): Call, as above, or Automatic, where it's called for you.
- **Stacking** (off): answer a Draw Two with another Draw Two, or either draw card with a Wild Draw Four (any time), to pass the total on. Whoever can't or won't answer draws it all. A Draw Two can't answer a Wild Draw Four.
- **Draw until playable** (off): keep drawing until you get a card you can play.
- **7-0 swaps** (off): playing a 7 swaps your hand with a player you pick; playing a 0 passes every hand on to the next player in the direction of play.
- **Jump-in** (off): if you hold a card identical to the top card (same colour and number or symbol, not a wild), play it out of turn. Play carries on from you. The fastest player wins the race.

## Tips

- Your playable cards are raised; the rest fade on your turn.
- The middle of the table shows the colour in play, the direction, and any stack waiting to be drawn.
- If a player leaves mid-round, their cards are shuffled into the draw pile.
- If you lose your connection on your turn, after 15 seconds you draw a card and your turn passes.
