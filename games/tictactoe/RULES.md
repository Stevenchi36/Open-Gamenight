# Tic-Tac-Toe

**Goal:** get three of your marks in a row before your opponent does.

## A game

1. One player is **X**, the other **O**. X goes first.
2. Take turns tapping an empty square to put your mark there.
3. Three in a row, across, down or diagonally, wins.
4. If all nine squares fill up with no line, it's a draw.

## Scoring

A win scores **1 point**; a draw scores nothing. Scores carry on from game to game.

## Next game

The host starts a new game. The players swap marks each game, so whoever went second last time goes first.

## Settings

None. Exactly 2 players.

## Tips

- The middle square is part of the most lines. Corners come next.
- If your opponent has two in a row, block the third square.
- The screen glows when it's your turn.
- If a player leaves mid-game, it ends with no winner.
