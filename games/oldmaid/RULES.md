# Old Maid

**Goal:** get rid of all your cards by making pairs, and don't be the one left holding the odd queen: the **Old Maid**.

## Setting up

The queen of clubs is taken out of the deck, so one queen has no partner. All 51 cards are dealt out. Any pairs in your hand go down straight away, by themselves.

## A pair

Two cards of the same rank, like two sevens. With **Match colour** on, they must be the same colour too: ♠ with ♣, or ♥ with ♦.

## Your turn

1. Take **one card, face down**, from the player before you (the next one anticlockwise who still has cards). You see only the backs: tap one, then **Take it**.
2. If it makes a pair, the pair goes down for everyone to see.
3. Then it's the next player's turn, and they take a card from you.

Only you and the player you took it from know which card it was, unless it made a pair.

## Going out

When your hand is empty, you're out and safe. The last player still holding a card has the odd queen: they're the **Old Maid**.

## Formats

- **Classic:** every round, everyone except the Old Maid scores **1 point**. Points carry on from round to round.
- **Royale:** the Old Maid is out of the Royale and sits out the next rounds. Keep playing until only one player is left: they win the Royale and score **1 point**. With 5 players that's 4 rounds.

## Next round

The host deals the next round. Everyone keeps their seat, and the first turn moves on one seat.

## Settings

- **Format** (default **Classic**): Classic or Royale, as above.
- **Match colour** (default off): pairs need the same rank and colour.
- **Arrange hand** (default off): you can change the order of your hand. Tap a card, then where it should go, or tap **Shuffle**.

Your hand is never sorted. It stays in the random order it was dealt, and a card you take lands in a random place. What you see is the order the next player picks from.

## Tips

- If you're holding the queen, the next player can take it from you, so keep a straight face.
- With **Arrange hand**, try moving the queen around. Or leave it in the same spot and hope they don't guess.
- If you're away when it's your turn, a card is taken for you at random after a few seconds.
- If a player leaves mid-round, their cards are dealt out to the others, so the queen stays in play.
- If you join mid-game, you're dealt in from the next round (in Royale, the next Royale).
