# Cheat

**Goal:** be the first to get rid of all your cards, by playing them face down and saying what they are. You don't have to tell the truth, but anyone can call **Cheat!**

## Setting up

The whole deck is dealt out, so everyone gets the same number of cards, give or take one. You see your own hand; for everyone else you see only how many cards they hold.

## Your turn

1. **Pick 1 to 4 cards** from your hand and play them face down onto the pile.
2. **Claim** they're all the rank it's time for: aces on the first turn, then twos, threes and so on up to kings, then aces again.
3. The number of cards is always true. The rank doesn't have to be: if you have no sevens when it's time for sevens, you'll have to bluff.

## Calling Cheat!

- Anyone except the player who just played can tap **Cheat!** until the next cards go down.
- After each play there's a short **call time** before the next player can play, so everyone gets a chance.
- The called cards are turned up for everyone to see:
  - **Any card isn't what was claimed:** the player who played them picks up the **whole pile**.
  - **They're all true:** the player who called picks up the whole pile.
- Either way, play carries on with the next player as before.

## Winning

When you play your last cards, you win once the call time runs out with nobody calling. If someone calls and you lied, you pick up the pile and play on. If you told the truth, you win. The winner gets **1 point**, and scores carry on from game to game.

## Next game

The host starts a new game. Everyone keeps their seat, and the first turn moves on one seat.

## Settings

- **Claims** (default **Next rank**): the claims go up one rank every turn. **Nearby**: claim the same rank as the last play, or one above or below (aces and kings count as neighbours); the first play of a game can claim any rank.
- **Call time** (default **5 seconds**): how long everyone gets to call after each play: 3, 5, 10 or 20 seconds. Pick 20 if anyone at the table needs longer to take in each play.
- **Move history** (default **Last 5**): how many past plays and calls you can look back at under **Moves this game**: **Off** (only the latest, so it's all memory), **Last 5**, or **All**.

## Tips

- Count what you hold: if you have three sevens, nobody else can truly play two.
- Play your bluffs early, while the pile is small and getting caught costs less.
- You can pick your cards while you wait for your turn.
- **Moves this game** lists recent plays and calls (as many as Move history allows), if you lose track.
- If a player leaves mid-game, their cards are set aside, and their last play can't be called any more.
