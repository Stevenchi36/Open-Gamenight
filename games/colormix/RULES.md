# Color Mix

**Goal:** guess the colour you'd get by mixing the paints on screen.

## A round

1. **Look at the colours.** Everyone sees the same 2 to 6 paint colours.
2. **Pick your guess in secret.** Use the colour picker to choose the colour they'd make if you mixed them **in equal parts, like paint**. Blue and yellow make green, red and white make pink. You can change your guess until the reveal.
3. **Reveal.** Once everyone has guessed (or the timer runs out, or the host taps **Reveal now**), the real mix appears next to every guess, closest first. Each guess shows how close it was, as a percentage to 2 decimal places: 100% is exact, and it drops quickly as guesses get further off, but never quite reaches 0.

## Scoring

The host picks one of two ways:

- **Ranked** (the default): you score **1 point for every player further away than you**. With 4 players, the closest gets 3, then 2, then 1, and the furthest gets 0. Equally close guesses score the same. No guess scores 0.
- **Closest only:** the closest guess scores **1 point**. If guesses tie for closest, they all score.

## Winning

- **Unlimited rounds** (the default): play as many rounds as you like; the scoreboard keeps count.
- **A set number of rounds:** after the last round, the highest score wins. Players tied at the top win together. The host can then start a new game.

## Settings

- **Rounds:** unlimited, or 5, 10, 15 or 20.
- **Colours to mix:** 2 to 6, or random (a new number each round).
- **Scoring:** ranked or closest only.
- **Guess timer:** off, or 30 to 120 seconds.

## Tips

- Paint gets darker and duller as you mix more colours in: with 5 or 6 colours, expect something murky.
- White only lightens: a colour mixed with white keeps its hue.
- Keep your screen hidden: your guess only shows while you hold it.
- The screen glows when the game is waiting for your guess.
- If you join mid-round, you play from the next round.
