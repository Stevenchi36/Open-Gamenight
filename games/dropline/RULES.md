# Drop Line

**Goal:** line up your discs (4 by default) before your opponent does.

## A game

1. One player is **red**, the other **blue**. Red goes first.
2. Take turns tapping a column to drop a disc into it. The disc falls to the lowest empty space.
3. A line of your discs, across, down or diagonally, as long as the game's line length (or longer) wins.
4. If the board fills up with no line, it's a draw.

## Scoring

A win scores **1 point**; a draw scores nothing. Scores carry on from game to game.

## Next game

The host starts a new game. The players swap colours each game, so whoever went second last time goes first.

## Settings

- **Line length:** 3 to 6 discs in a row (default 4).
- **Board size:** small, standard (default) or large. The columns and rows depend on the line length:

| Line | Small | Standard | Large |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 4×4 | 5×4 | 6×5 |
| 4 | 6×5 | 7×6 | 8×7 |
| 5 | 8×7 | 9×7 | 10×8 |
| 6 | 9×8 | 10×9 | 11×10 |

(columns × rows)

## Tips

- Middle columns are part of the most lines, so they're worth taking early.
- Watch for a disc that would land just under your opponent's winning space: playing there lets them drop on top.
- The screen glows when it's your turn, and the last disc dropped is marked.
- If a player leaves mid-game, it ends with no winner.
