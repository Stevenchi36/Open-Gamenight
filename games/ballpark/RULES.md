# Ballpark

**Goal:** score the most points by guessing numbers and, more importantly, by betting on whose guess is best. You don't need to know the answers: a good bettor can beat a know-it-all.

## A round

1. **Read the question.** Everyone sees the same one, like "How tall is the Eiffel Tower?", with the unit to answer in.
2. **Guess in secret.** Type a number. You can change it until the guesses are revealed.
3. **The board.** Once everyone has guessed (or the timer runs out, or the host taps **Reveal guesses**), the guesses go on the board from lowest to highest. Equal guesses share a spot. With the **without going over** rule there's also an **All too high** spot, for when every guess is above the answer.
4. **Bet.** Everyone places **2 tokens** on the board, on any guesses, both on one if you like. You can move them until the answer is revealed.
5. **The answer.** Once everyone has bet (or the timer runs out, or the host taps **Reveal the answer**), the answer appears and the winning guess lights up.

## Which guess wins

- **Without going over** (the default): the highest guess that isn't above the answer. If every guess is too high, **All too high** wins.
- **Closest:** the guess nearest the answer, above or below. Two guesses equally far away both win.

## Odds

Each spot pays **odds**: the middle guess pays **×2**, and each step towards the edges pays one more, up to **×5**. **All too high** pays **×6**. Long shots pay more.

## Scoring

- **Odds** (the default): each token on the winning guess scores its odds. Writing the winning guess scores **3**.
- **Chips:** your score is your pile of chips. When you bet, you can stake chips on each token. A winning token pays its odds × (1 + stake); a losing token loses its stake. A token with no stake costs nothing, so you can always play. Writing the winning guess scores **3**.
- **Flat:** each token on the winning guess scores **1**, and writing it scores **1**.

## Winning

- **Rounds** (7 by default): the highest score after the last question wins; ties win together. The host can then start a new game.
- **Unlimited:** play as many questions as you like; the scoreboard keeps count.

## Settings

- **Rounds:** 5, 7, 10 or 15 questions, or unlimited.
- **Units:** metric or imperial. Answers are converted and rounded, and the rounded number is the one that counts.
- **Win rule:** without going over, or closest.
- **Scoring:** odds, chips or flat.
- **Bets:** hidden (you only see who has bet) or public (tokens show as they land).
- **Questions from:** all (least-played first), or recent (the newest questions added first, then older ones).
- **Category:** any, or one category of questions.
- **Timer:** off, or 30 to 120 seconds for guessing and again for betting.

## Tips

- Guessing low is safer with the without going over rule.
- If someone at the table knows the topic, bet on their guess.
- Keep your screen hidden: your guess (and hidden bets) only show while you hold them.
- The screen glows when the game is waiting for your guess or your bets.
- If you join mid-round, you play from the next question.
